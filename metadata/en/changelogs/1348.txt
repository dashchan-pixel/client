* Posting: the form is a sheet over the thread instead of replacing it — drag it down or swipe it away to read what you are answering (the full-screen form is User interface → "Classic style") — and a captcha you just solved is reused for two minutes instead of being solved again on every open.
* A floating bar puts the toolbar's actions within reach of the thumb. Long press to drag and reorder, two-finger tap to turn it vertical.
* Captcha: reCAPTCHA 3 — no challenge, the token is minted in the background inside the site's own page.
* Every forum keeps a ban log (board, reason, expiration, address), and no captcha is spent on a post it says will be refused.
* Visible IP: a command flagged "Run to change visible IP" runs from the forum's settings, a banned post's toast or a script. No proxy service has code in the app — Asocks and DataImpulse ship as commands in docs/commands.
* "Proxy for sending only": posts and reports take the proxy, browsing and files go direct. A proxy takes a login and a password now.
* Commands can be granted the app's settings, the forum and its cookies, their own key→value store and — apart — the visible IP and proxy; they can ask the user (show, confirm, prompt, choose, chooseMany, with alert/confirm/prompt bound to real dialogs instead of being cancelled unasked); and a new App target takes nothing, works through its grants and is what the app runs when it needs another visible IP.
* Toasts stack up to three instead of covering one another.
* 4chan hands out captchas again: header lookups missed on HTTP/2 hosts, leaving a Cloudflare challenge unrecognised. Its "Verification Required" title is recognised too.
* A forum's Connection settings take a User-Agent of their own.
* References to deleted posts are links now, marked X, opening the thread's last-seen copy instead of a 404.
* Threadshots no longer crash on a post with a country flag or an extension's icon.
* Extensions: a drag-slider captcha, and a TAG_SECRET markup tag with a posting-form button.
